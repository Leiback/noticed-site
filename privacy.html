Privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026

The short version: Noticed does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you write stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your notes, what you need, your reflections, photos you attach, your partner's and supporters' names and numbers, and your settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. Noticed has no servers, so we never receive them and cannot read them.

Deleting a note removes it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

The on-device assist

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Noticed can suggest wordings, read back a note and draft thank-yous. This runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model. Your words are not sent to us, to Apple or to anyone else for this.

Voice

If you speak a note instead of typing it, it is transcribed on your iPhone. Audio is not recorded or sent anywhere.

When you send a message

Noticed never sends anything on its own. When you choose to send, Noticed hands the exact text you approved to the app you picked: Messages, WhatsApp, or another app through the iOS share sheet. From that point the message is handled by that app and its own privacy policy, as with any message you write there.

Contacts and photos

"Choose from Contacts" uses Apple's contact picker. Noticed receives only the name and number of the one person you pick, and never has access to the rest of your contacts. Photos you attach are chosen through Apple's photo picker and copied into the app's own storage on your iPhone.

Face ID

If you turn on "Require Face ID to open", Face ID is handled by iOS. Noticed only learns whether the check succeeded and never has access to your face data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. They never include your words or your partner's name.

Backups

If you export a backup, it is encrypted on your iPhone with a passcode you choose, and saved wherever you put it (for example Files or iCloud Drive). We never receive it and cannot open it.

Purchases

The free week and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Noticed only learns whether you have the free week or the purchase, never your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Information from Apple

Apple may share anonymous, aggregated statistics with developers, such as downloads and, if you opted in, crash reports. These do not identify you and do not include anything you wrote.

Children

Noticed is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Noticed will never start collecting your notes or messages.
