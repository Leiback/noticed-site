The free week and the purchase

How does pricing work?

Everything in Noticed is free for your first 7 days. If you'd like to keep it, it's one payment. No subscription, and nothing to pay to start the free week.

What happens after the 7 days if I don't buy it?

Everything you wrote stays on your iPhone to read, search, export and delete. Writing new notes and sending pause until you keep Noticed.

I bought it, or reinstalled, and it's asking again.

Open You in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I have a code.

Tap Have a code? wherever the offer appears, or redeem it in the App Store app under your account.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sending messages

Does my partner need the app?

No. Messages arrive as ordinary texts in whatever app you send with.

How do I send with WhatsApp or another app?

Go to You → Partner → Send with and choose Messages, WhatsApp or Another app. "Another app" opens the share sheet so you can pick LINE, WeChat, Telegram, Signal or anything else. You can also tap Copy message and paste it anywhere.

WhatsApp can't find the person.

WhatsApp needs the full number with its country code, like +1 415 555 0182. Edit the number in You, or use Choose from Contacts.

Does Noticed send anything by itself?

Never. You always see the exact words first, and the message leaves only when you send it from your messaging app.

The on-device assist

Which iPhones have it?

The assist needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On other iPhones, Noticed offers built-in gentler wordings instead.

Does it work in my language?

The app is in English, but the assist replies in the language you write in, for languages Apple Intelligence supports.

Privacy and your data

Where are my notes?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

In You → Back up or export your data, choose a passcode and save the encrypted file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, use Restore from a backup. Keep the passcode safe: nobody can recover it, including us.

Can I lock or disguise the app?

Yes. Turn on Require Face ID to open, and choose a different home-screen icon under How Noticed looks on your home screen.

How do I delete everything?

Delete individual notes from the note sheet, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
